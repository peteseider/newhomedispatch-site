Permanent Rate Promotions vs. Market

One permanent-rate promo remains flagged in today's sweep: Stylecraft Homes' “Season of Savings” offer — 5.25% (6.017% APR), explicitly described as a 30-year fixed FHA rate (not an ARM, not labeled temporary or introductory), paid for by Stylecraft via its featured lender, Kangaroo Home Lending, on new contracts closing by December 28, 2026. This is its 3rd consecutive day in this table since first being flagged on 2026-11-16. We are still treating this as unconfirmed with the builder, not yet verified as a true standing 30-year-fixed program. Today's market benchmark is 7.11% (Freddie Mac PMMS, 30-year fixed, print dated 2026-11-12). A rate that undercuts that benchmark by more than a point, without clear life-of-loan language in writing, is very likely a temporary buydown or an ARM — ask your lender what the rate becomes after the buydown period or ARM reset, not just the headline number.

Buyer News: Foreclosure Looms for Georgetown Housing Project With Unfinished Homes — Austin American-Statesman, corroborated by KXAN and KVUE. The planned 921-home Rocking Wilco subdivision in Georgetown, built by Onx Homes using a modular/tech-forward method, has sat with dozens of unfinished, abandoned homes since construction halted roughly nine months ago and is now headed to a foreclosure auction after no buyer stepped in. It's a direct illustration of why a builder's financial stability and completed track record matter as much as price or incentives: before contracting with any builder, especially a newer or smaller entrant, ask how long they've operated in this market, whether they've completed a prior subdivision start-to-finish, and how your deposit is protected if construction stalls.
