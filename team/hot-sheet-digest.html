What Moved Today

  • Tri Pointe Homes cut its headline incentive across six communities. The builder relabeled its promotion "The Value of Now Sales Event" and lowered the offer from $30,000 + a 5.25% rate to $10,000 + a 4.99% rate at Park Central, Lagos Reserve, Lariat, Flora, Heritage, and Turner's Crossing (plus a renamed seventh listing, Union Collection at Park Central, formerly "Paired Homes at Park Central"). Edgewood ($40K closing credit), Noven ($50K closing credit), Capitol Collection at Lariat ($35K closing credit), and Wolf Ranch ($30K/5.75% choice) are unchanged. This is today's single largest confirmed incentive move, in either direction.
  • Century Communities' rate moved up from 3.875% (4.735% APR) to 4.25% (5.119% APR) on its "Wow Doesn't Wait" promo page, still adjusting after the first 5 years through Inspire Home Loans.
  • Pacesetter Homes is back in Buyer Beware. Its page's own fine-print "close by October 15, 2026" disclaimer has now passed, joining the long-running three-way deadline conflict (Nov 3 banner vs. Oct 15 close-by vs. a May FAQ window) we've flagged on and off for the past several weeks.
  • Corridor coverage held at 18/19 for a second consecutive run. Taylor Morrison remains the sole unreachable corridor builder (7th+ consecutive run with an empty, JS-rendered page); Ash Creek Homes' title-policy closing-cost offer was reconfirmed directly on its Drew Lane community page.
  • Freddie Mac's benchmark rate held at 7.40% for a 7th consecutive reading of the same weekly print (the next print is due on or about Oct 22).
  • Methodology note on today's median: DRB Homes' Flex Cash tiers were captured as 31 separate per-community rows this run (full itemization), versus a handful of consolidated tiers recently. That capture change, not any actual market pullback, is the main reason today's median fell to $20,000 from $30,000 — see the Buyer Beware / footnote below before reading this as builders broadly retreating.
  • Flintrock Builders' two domains still disagree on rate (4.49% on the primary site vs. 2.99%/4.99% on the landing-page subdomain, and a third figure of 3.99% in the primary page's own meta description), now roughly 24 runs unresolved.

Buyer News

Why Buying a New Home Is Now Cheaper Than an Existing One in Many Markets — FOX 7 Austin. Market data shows the long-standing new-construction price premium over resale has narrowed or inverted in several metros, including Austin, as builders lean on rate buydowns, price cuts, and closing-cost credits. Buyer takeaway: before assuming a resale home is cheaper, get the builder's current incentive-adjusted price in writing and compare total cost, not just list price.
