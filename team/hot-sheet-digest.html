Permanent Rate Promotions vs. Market

No permanent-rate promotions are confirmed live today — 11th consecutive run at zero. We report a permanent-rate promotion only when a builder's own page or paperwork uses language like "permanent" or "life of the loan," and we say so plainly when that bar isn't met. Regency by Toll Brothers' 5.49% rate was rechecked again today on both its main page and Garden Collection sub-page and still carries no such language. MileStone Community Builders' "6% MileStone credit" buydown to 4.75%–4.99% fixed for 30 years remains held in Buyer Beware because the only page describing it carries a passed deadline (October 31, 2025, on 2026-branded content).

Freddie Mac PMMS 30-year benchmark: 7.32%, held from the 2026-10-22 print. A $400,000 loan at 7.32% vs. a builder-advertised 4.99%–5.50% rate runs roughly $400–$550 less per month while the lower rate is in effect — always confirm in writing whether a builder-advertised rate is temporary, an ARM, or tied to a required affiliate lender before treating it as your long-term payment.

Why Buying a New Home Is Now Cheaper Than an Existing One in Many Markets — FOX 7 Austin. Market data shows the long-standing new-construction price premium over resale has narrowed or inverted in several metros, including Austin, as builders lean on rate buydowns, price cuts, and closing-cost credits.
