CEO Strategy

Vision, mission, and the three prongs of the business

This section is written for the CEO, who is also the one employee. Read it the way you would read the first three slides of a pitch to an investor, because that is exactly what it is. Everything below it in this book is the operating detail underneath these few paragraphs.

Vision

Become the one name in Central Texas new construction that buyers trust more than the builder, more than the portal, and more than the sales rep standing in the model home, and use that trust to build the recurring, defensible research business other agents cannot copy.

Mission

Do the homework buyers cannot do themselves, publish it for free before anyone asks, and turn every person who reads it into either a client, a subscriber, or a referral, without ever putting coverage up for sale.

Operating principle

Anything done more than once becomes a system in this book. If a call, a text, a form, or a link gets used twice, it gets written down here so it never has to be reinvented a third time.

The three prongs

Every asset, every page, every dollar in this business belongs to one of three prongs. They are not three separate businesses, they are three stages of the same person moving from stranger to client.

1. New Home Dispatch, the trust engine

Independent research, free tools, and field reporting on new construction builders across the metro. This is the front door. Nobody pays for coverage, nobody gets special treatment, and that independence is exactly what makes it worth reading. Its only job is to earn trust before anyone is asked for anything.

2. PeteSeider.com, the conversion engine

This is where a reader becomes a lead. The buyer and seller questionnaires, the advantage pages, the true cost tools, and the buyer strategy call booking all live here. It is the personal brand site, and it is currently the main business, the one that actually puts food on the table. Every other prong exists to feed it traffic it can convert.

3. Seider Realty Team, the production engine

Powered by The Ascend Group at LPT Realty. This is where a lead becomes a closing, touring, negotiating, and getting to the closing table. It is the only prong where a license is required and the only place commission is earned. Say the affiliation line exactly this way when it comes up: "Seider Realty Team, powered by The Ascend Group at LPT Realty."

Why three prongs and not one: a portal lead does not trust a salesperson on first contact, but they will trust a researcher. New Home Dispatch earns the trust a direct sales pitch cannot. PeteSeider.com converts that trust into an actual lead with contact information and intent. Seider Realty Team closes it. Skipping a step, pitching representation before the trust is earned, is the single most common way a good lead goes cold.

How leads become closings, the funnel

Three sources bring people in. One trust engine warms them up. One site converts them. One team closes them. This is the whole business drawn as a diagram, and it is the map every script and every tool in this book plugs into somewhere.

Where people come from

Zillow & portals

Inbound calls and leads from Zillow, the paid, lowest trust, highest volume source. They do not know Pete yet.

The database

About 400 past clients and contacts. The highest trust source, and the one most often under used between transactions.

Social media

Organic content and DMs, medium trust, and the audience most likely to have already read something from New Home Dispatch before they ever say hello.

Where they go next

New Home Dispatch

Free tools and independent research build trust fast, the incentive tracker, the true cost calculator, the communities tool, the Friday email.

The part that makes this different from a normal agent's funnel: a normal agent tries to convert a Zillow lead directly into a client on the first call, and it rarely works because there is no trust yet. NHD exists to shorten that gap. Sending a Zillow caller to a free, independent tool the same day does more to build trust in ten minutes than three follow up calls do, and it is the reason a New Home Dispatch link belongs in almost every first contact script in this book.

The moat, why this is hard to copy

Straight from the research strategy: nearly every competitor reports price, the slowest, most lagging number in housing. New Home Dispatch's edge is publishing the indicators that move before price does, sales, listings, permits, and rates, so it describes where the market is going while everyone else describes where it has already been. That edge is what makes the free content worth reading, which is what makes the whole funnel above work.

  • Proprietary composites. The NHD Leverage Index and similar named, owned indexes are citation magnets nobody else can publish, because nobody else built them.
  • The new construction lane. Builder incentives, true cost versus resale, and buydown math, the exact area most national price outlets ignore because new homes are a small share of total sales.
  • Relocation intelligence. Migration data framed as a decision tool for the exact audience that already defines both New Home Dispatch and Seider Realty Team, people moving in.
  • The Austin flagship. Being the definitive research voice on the one market this audience cares about most, before expanding the same machine to any other metro.

Memorize the ten second version. Everything else builds on it. Every script below has a copy button so you can paste it straight into a text or say it back word for word until it feels natural in your own voice.

Ten seconds

It's called New Home Dispatch. Think of it as an independent guide for new construction buyers. Free tools, real field reporting, no builder spin. I'm Pete Seider. I built it because buyers deserve someone doing the homework on their side.

Thirty seconds

New Home Dispatch is a consumer publication for people buying new construction homes in Central Texas. We visit the communities ourselves, track builder incentives week over week, and verify the tax rates builders tend to gloss over. Then we publish it all for free: a true cost calculator, an offer checker, a twenty five point tour checklist. It's independent, which means coverage is never for sale. If someone wants hands on help beyond the research, that's the Seider Realty Team, powered by The Ascend Group at LPT Realty. The tools stay free for everybody either way, no strings attached.

Two minutes, five beats in order

Origin. Years of walking buyers through contracts, watching them get surprised at the design center, by MUD taxes, by "from" prices that never seem to apply to them.

The difference. Builder claims get labeled as claims, and when we're wrong, we correct it publicly.

The products. The Explorer, every community verified in person. The Friday scoreboard. The free tools.

What's next. Field verification is ongoing, the builder directory keeps growing, more markets are planned.

The ask. Their story, or someone they know who's shopping.

Reasons to reach out. Always have a real one, never just checking in.

  • The story ask, for past clients: "I'm building something and I want your voice in it."

Qualify & capture

Goal: get the handful of facts that decide everything else, on the same call, without it feeling like an interrogation. Use the in-call capture form in section 19 so nothing gets forgotten and it is ready to paste straight into Follow Up Boss.

Call, the five questions

A few quick questions so I send you the right stuff and don't waste your time. Are you further along and ready to tour, or still early in the research? Is this new construction, resale, or open to either? What area or areas are you focused on? Do you have a timeline, are we talking weeks or months? And have you talked to a lender yet, or is that still ahead of you?

  • Stage: early research, actively touring, or ready to write an offer. This decides everything sent in the next stage.
  • Product: new construction, resale, or open to either. New construction routes to the Incentive Tracker and the new construction advantage page. Resale routes to the buyer advantage page and the communities tool.
  • Area: specific community or general part of town. Determines which community page or guide to send.
  • Timeline: weeks, months, or just watching. Sets the nurture cadence, see stage 4.
  • Financing: pre approved, talking to a lender, or has not started. Someone with no lender yet needs that conversation before anything else moves.
Open the capture form now: jump to In-Call Capture Forms and fill it out live while you are still on the call. It builds a clean summary you can copy straight into Follow Up Boss when you hang up.

The observation formula, what goes in "Three Things I Saw"

What, plus where or scope, plus a number, plus how you know. Then tag it:

  • Verified, you counted it, photographed it, or hold the document.
  • Builder claimed, a rep or flyer said it, treat it as a claim.
  • Needs verification, conflicting, verbal only, or unchecked.

Good: "Six completed spec homes on Serene Way, two with price improved riders, counted and photographed."  ·  Bad: "Lots of inventory sitting." No number, no location, no evidence.

Business Systems, NHD

Data & Research Architecture

The short version of how NHD's research actually gets built, for anyone who needs to understand why the numbers are trustworthy, not just what they say.

Entity ID system

Every builder, community, tax district, property, source, offer, observation, and publication has a stable ID. This is what stops "D.R. Horton" and "DR Horton" from silently becoming two different builders in the data.

Coverage minimums

Before any metro wide claim is published: at least fifteen active builders, at least forty communities, all five MSA counties, at least eighty percent verified with a quote, at least ninety percent observed within thirty days. Until that bar is met, everything is labeled pilot or sample, never a metro wide claim.

Evidence tiers

Public builder claim, then MLS, then direct written confirmation, then field observation, then verified transaction. Field talk is never treated as equal to written terms.

MLS and portal data, never scraped

Under any circumstance. This protects the pending MLS Grid and IDX application, which is an existential risk to a real business relationship, not just a style choice.

Onboarding

Rules That Do Not Change

  • Free tools first, always. Representation is a separate, optional door, never lead with it.
  • Do not quote a number from memory. Incentives and tax rates move weekly, point to the live tracker or calculator instead.
  • MLS and portal data is never scraped, under any circumstance. It protects the real IDX relationship.
  • One good field capture beats five rushed ones. A visit is not complete until the data is entered, not when you leave the community.
  • Builder claims are claims until verified. Advertised prices are floors, not facts.
  • Byline standard: Pete Seider or the reporter's real name, never anonymous when representing the brand.
  • Affiliation line, said exactly this way when it comes up: "Seider Realty Team, powered by The Ascend Group at LPT Realty."
  • One brand per asset, never both. See The Two Brands.
