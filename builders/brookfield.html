Brookfield Residential

Brookfield Residential is unusual in this dataset: it is both the master-plan developer and a direct homebuilder in the same two Austin-area communities. This profile is built from the builder's own site and the same daily incentive-tracking behind the Hot Sheet — not a field visit yet. See exactly what we've verified below.

On the record

This profile previously listed Brookfield as active in only 1 tracked community (Kissing Tree). Our own incentive-tracking data has been logging Brookfield offers tied to Easton Park for weeks, and the builder's own site confirms Brookfield builds directly in both communities — so we've added Easton Park as a second tracked community here. Separately, Easton Park's own community profile page lists its tracked builders as David Weekley, Highland Homes, Pacesetter Homes, Perry Homes, and Taylor Morrison, and does not currently include Brookfield — that page's builder list needs the same correction in a future pass. We're noting the gap rather than silently fixing it here, since it's a change to a different page.

What we can verify — and what we haven't
✅ Verified by us

Brookfield builds directly (not just develops) in both Easton Park and Kissing Tree, confirmed on the builder's own Austin-area page. The 67 quick-move-in count and both starting prices, counted directly on that same page. The March 2015 Grand Haven Homes acquisition that brought Brookfield into the Central Texas market.

🟡 Builder claims, not independently confirmed

The $4,600 design credit and 1.99% temporary rate buydown — both logged from builder-reported sourcing, neither re-confirmed since late July. Brookfield's promotions page is JS-rendered and didn't return readable offer terms during this research pass, so we could not do a same-day re-check the way we did for some other builders.

🟠 Not yet checked

Construction quality and sales-office practices firsthand — no field visit yet. BBB record for the Texas entity. Whether a captive mortgage or title affiliate exists but simply wasn't surfaced on the pages we reviewed.

Ask this on your visit: At both Easton Park and Kissing Tree, ask directly whether you're comparing a Brookfield-built home against a different builder's section of the same community — since Brookfield is also the developer, sales staff may frame comparisons differently than they would at a community they don't own. Confirm the design credit and rate buydown are still active; both were last confirmed by us in late July.

Coverage

Kissing Tree (San Marcos) sits inside New Home Dispatch's South Austin/Kyle/Buda/San Marcos priority corridor; Easton Park (Southeast Austin/Manor) does not. Brookfield's dual role as developer and builder in both communities makes it worth a closer look for anyone comparing prices within either community, since the price the developer sets for land and amenities affects every builder there, including itself.

The bottom line

Advantage: Brookfield is a well-capitalized, decades-old developer-builder with a direct presence in a priority-corridor community (Kissing Tree, San Marcos), a competitively priced entry point at Easton Park ($314,990), and full control over both land development and homebuilding in its communities.

Limitation: its dual developer/builder role means it isn't a fully independent comparison point against the other builders sharing its own communities, its two tracked incentives haven't been re-confirmed since late July, and no captive lender or title affiliate is currently visible to us for a financing comparison.
