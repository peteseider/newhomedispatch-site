Advertised vs. Achieved · Now tracking

The advertised deal is the starting point. The achieved deal is the truth.

A builder’s advertisement tells you what they want the market to know. The final contract tells you what they were actually willing to do. Extra closing costs. A lot premium quietly waived. A rate bought lower than the flyer said. Texas doesn’t make sold prices public, so this record has never existed anywhere. We’re building it, one verified report at a time.

Step 01

Reported

A buyer or agent submits the real deal structure in about three minutes. Mostly taps and dropdowns, no essay required.

Step 02

Verified

We review, redact everything personal, and label the evidence honestly, from contributor reported to document verified.

Step 03

Published

Anonymized deal examples join the record, so the next buyer walks in knowing what has actually been done.

Report a deal → How verification works →
The record is open. The first verified deals are being logged now.

You’ll see individual verified examples before you see averages. We don’t publish statistics until the sample is real, and every number carries a label telling you exactly how it’s known. Reports are anonymous, published at community and month level only.
